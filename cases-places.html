Direction for stillness.

Places

A quiet space disguised as a coloring book.

The Challenge

Modern life rarely leaves room for quiet moments. I wanted to explore whether design could encourage people to slow down, reconnect with the present, and experience creativity without pressure or performance.

The Approach

Rather than creating a traditional coloring book, I designed a collection of illustrations inspired by peaceful places and everyday beauty. Every composition was carefully balanced to feel inviting, calming, and enjoyable to color, encouraging a slower, more mindful creative experience.

The Outcome

The project became more than a coloring book. It became an exploration of how illustration, composition, and visual storytelling can create moments of presence, relaxation, and emotional connection.
