Filosofia de Design

What I Believe.

No que Acredito.

These are not rules. They are the questions I come back to every time I design something.

Estas não são regras. São as perguntas para as quais volto sempre que desenho algo.

Technology should simplify.
Never intimidate.
If a user hesitates, the interface has failed.
People don't need more options.
They need better guidance.
Great design isn't about increasing possibilities. It's about helping people choose with confidence.
Every interaction should answer a question.
Every screen should reduce uncertainty. Every decision should feel easier than the previous one.
Branding is not decoration.
It's decision making.
A brand is a system of choices that builds trust and clarity.
When design is working, people stop noticing the interface and start trusting themselves.
